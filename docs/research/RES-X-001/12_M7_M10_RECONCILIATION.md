# 12 M7 × M10 Reconciliation

## Attention boundary

`Visual attention ≠ Cognition authority ≠ World authority`。

| User signal               | M7 effect                                     | M10 effect                                      | Forbidden effect                             |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| AOI/visibility            | raise V LOD, stream or animate representation | none by itself                                  | changing resident rights or facts            |
| select/follow resident    | presentation emphasis                         | bounded attention bonus only if policy allows   | unlimited I3                                 |
| direct human conversation | richer presentation                           | optional interaction cognition budget           | bypassing Proxy/Kernel authority             |
| repeated camera switching | no durable world change                       | rate-limited, resident-scoped, auditable signal | silently transferring budget from all others |

## Fairness risk

M5's older score gives `P_user` high weight. M10 corrects this with `fairness.quota` and `world.cap`, but the exact budget owner and accounting identity are pending. Without a resident/world/window cap, a user can cycle attention across residents and indirectly create unbounded I2/I3 work. This is an abuse/fairness risk, not a visual feature.

## Finding

`PARTIALLY_ALIGNED` with `C4` risk. M7 may optimize what is seen; M10 may give a bounded, optional cognition bonus; neither may alter Kernel validation or world authority. Future test must compare attention-on/off fact hashes and prove no cross-resident or cross-world budget leakage.
